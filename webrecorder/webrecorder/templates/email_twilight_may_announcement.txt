Conifer is currently in the process of entering its Twilight Phase--read more about the reasoning in the original announcement from December 2025 (https://blog.conifer.rhizome.org/2025/12/15/twilight-announcement.html).

The process is divided into 3 steps:

  [complete] December 2025: The creation of new accounts and subscriptions is closed.

  [complete] May 2026: Paying users' subscriptions are stopped, with a pro-rated refund.

  [to do]    June 2026: The Conifer platform is replaced with a "tombstone app" that will allow continued hosting of collections with Rhizome, move them to the Browsertrix service (https://webrecorder.net/browsertrix/), self-host, or delete.
